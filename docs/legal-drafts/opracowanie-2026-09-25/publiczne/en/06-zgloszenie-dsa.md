# Report illegal content or a breach of the rules

Reporting to {{OPERATOR_NAME}} is free and does not require an account. Form: {{DSA_FORM_URL}}; alternatively email {{DSA_EMAIL}}. In an immediate threat to life or safety, also contact the appropriate emergency services; this form is not an emergency service.

Provide the exact address or identifier of the content and explain why you believe it is illegal. You may identify the country and legal provision but do not need to know an article number. Categories: fraud or false vacancy; demands for data or fees; human trafficking or forced labour; discrimination; unlawful working conditions; privacy infringement; threats or harassment; sexual offences involving children; third-party rights; impersonation; other illegality. You may also explain and report a breach of the terms alone.

Give your name or organisation name and contact email. These are not required in cases covered by Article 16(2)(c) DSA concerning offences under Articles 3–7 of Directive 2011/93/EU. Do not send copies of illegal sexual material involving children; provide its location and a description. Attachments are optional.

Statement: **“I confirm my good-faith belief that the information and allegations provided are accurate and complete.”**

We acknowledge receipt without undue delay where contact information is available and communicate the decision and available remedies. We aim to handle ordinary cases within 7 calendar days and urgent cases without delay. This is not a statutory period permitting illegal content to remain available. A free appeal is available for 6 calendar months after notification of the decision; we aim to respond within 14 days. Applicable DSA remedies, a complaint to the coordinator and court proceedings remain available. Belgium’s coordinator is BIPT/IBPT.

Data is used to handle the case, demonstrate accountability and meet applicable legal obligations; details: {{PRIVACY_URL}}. We do not automatically publish a reporter’s identity or disclose it to the author without assessing necessity and legal grounds. We retain the case during handling and the appeal window, then generally for 365 days after the later of the window closing or a timely appeal ending. A legal proceeding may justify a limited exception.
