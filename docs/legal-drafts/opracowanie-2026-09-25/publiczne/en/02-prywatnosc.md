## Retention

We delete CVs after 365 days of genuine inactivity and accounts and profiles after 730 days, with a warning 30 days beforehand. A voluntarily searchable profile is hidden after 180 days of inactivity. Applications and related messages are generally retained for 180 days after the process closes; independent threads for 180 days after the last substantive message. Genuinely ongoing processes are not closed solely because of a technical access event or its absence.

An unverified guest application is kept for no more than 7 days from the first submission; the verification link is valid for 48 hours. After verification, application data moves to the relevant process and a minimal confirmation record is retained for up to 30 days. Account linking is available for no more than 30 days and does not automatically enable searchability.

Raw security logs: up to 30 days; minimised audit records: up to 365 days. Email-queue content: generally up to 30 days after the final delivery outcome; minimal metadata and notifications: up to 90 days. Access secrets have shorter expiry periods. DSA cases: during handling and the appeal window, then 365 days after the later of the window closing or a timely appeal being resolved. Minimal evidence of consent, rights handling and accountability: up to 1095 days from the relevant closure or withdrawal. A minimal suppression record is kept as long as necessary to respect your decision not to receive messages, with annual review.

Analytics event identifiers: up to 48 hours; vacancy statistics: up to 13 calendar months, with safeguards for small groups. Backups are no more than 14 calendar days old. Minimal technical deletion records are kept for 30 days to prevent deleted data being restored from backups.

Following an erasure request, access is cut off without undue delay; our target is physical deletion of online files within 72 hours. Routine cleanup of files already marked for deletion is completed within 7 days. Backups are isolated and expire within the stated period; deletions are reapplied before a restored service is reopened. A specific legal obligation or dispute may justify keeping only the necessary data longer. The company may have its own separately justified retention of the received application.
