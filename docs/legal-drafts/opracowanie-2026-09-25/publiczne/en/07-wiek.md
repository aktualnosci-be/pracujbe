# Age and younger candidates’ safety

Public vacancies can be viewed without an account. A self-service Pracuj.be account is intended for people aged 16 or over. This is a service policy, not a statement that everyone of that age may perform every job.

If you are 16 or 17, involve or obtain permission from a parent or guardian where required by the law governing your capacity to contract. Your profile will not appear in companies’ candidate search. We do not enable optional analytics or marketing for users we know are under 18. We do not routinely ask for identity-document scans.

If you are under 16, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} together with your guardian to discuss an appropriate lawful support route. Do not provide an incorrect age. Account restrictions do not remove data protection rights: contact {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}, including without a guardian where the law permits you to act independently.

The employer must check youth-employment rules for the country and role, including schooling requirements, permitted work and working time. Do not send money, identity documents or private photographs to an unfamiliar person. Report suspicious vacancies through {{DSA_FORM_URL}}. Consent to an online service, eligibility for an account and lawful eligibility for employment are separate matters.
